01 · Local AI operating system

Z: Local AI Assistant

A local-first personal AI operating system for Windows, with a FastAPI backend, React + Vite + Tailwind dashboard, SQLite storage, and optional local LLMs through Ollama. Its dashboard brings together Chat, Life OS, Calendar + Workflow, Reports, Entry Log, System, Settings, and Notes. Actions require an exact preview and explicit approval.

  • Life OS includes a daily planner based on C.O.O.L. and a Projects map
  • System includes PC health and maintenance
